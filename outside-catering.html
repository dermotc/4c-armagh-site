We arrive, set up, serve, and clean up

Planning an event is stressful enough without worrying about who's plating the food. Tell us where and when, and we'll suggest a menu or build one around what you already have in mind — so you get to attend your own party.

We've catered Olympic homecomings, weddings, conferences, company training days and a great many birthdays. Multi-day and multi-sitting events are no trouble; tell us the numbers and the dietary requirements and we'll do the rest.

What's included
  • Set up, including tables and staging for an empty room
  • Food, utensils and crockery
  • Serving staff for the duration
  • Hot and cold refreshments from registration onwards
  • Every dietary requirement catered for
  • Clean up afterwards

Hot dishes run from lasagne and curries to beef stroganoff, chicken goujons and a range of salads. Finger food from spring rolls and cocktail sausages to full sandwich platters.
