Quality food in a quirky, cosy setting

The one that started it. Two floors and an all-weather yard, close enough to both cathedrals that the walk back up counts as your exercise.

Breakfast from eight, lunch from twelve, an after-school menu built for the school run, and Nite Bites on Friday and Saturday where you bring your own bottle and we charge a small corkage. Dogs are welcome in the pet-friendly areas. If you're in a hurry, order at the hatch.

New this summer: our deli counter is open — freshly prepared sandwiches, homemade salads, wraps, sausage rolls, artisan treats and hot and cold drinks, ready to grab when time isn't on your side. And if your favourite isn't in the counter, anything on the menu can be made to take away.

The room is also for hire — three spaces, a hundred and thirty seats between them.
