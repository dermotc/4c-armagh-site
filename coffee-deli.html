Thank you, Market Square

Opened in 2025 as 4C The Bakery and later trading as 4C Coffee & Deli, the shop at 15 Market Square has now closed. Same counter, same people, two names — and a lot of good mornings.

Nothing about it is gone. Coffee pulled properly, sandwiches made to order and traybakes that slow the walk back to the office are all on at the Coffee House & Kitchen, 2 College Hill — and anything on the menu there can be made to take away.

Office platters are still on. Give us two hours' notice at College Hill and they will be ready when you arrive.

Feeding a bigger room? Outside catering travels anywhere you need us.
