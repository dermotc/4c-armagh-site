Who you'll meet

The team is the reason

The food is what gets people through the door the first time. What brings them back is the person who carried the tray over, remembered how they take it, and asked after the dog.

Our staff have been with us a long time, and it shows in the small things — the table that's ready before you ask, the high chair that appears without a fuss, the regular whose usual is already going on. We hire for warmth first and train the rest.

Dogs are welcome too. Take yours for a stroll, meet friends, and sit in our pet-friendly areas at College Hill — there's water waiting.
